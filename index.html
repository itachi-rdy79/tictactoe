👑 How to Play Ludo King

🎯 Game Objective: Be the first player to race all 4 tokens around the 52-square track and into your center Home (👑).

🎲 Unlocking Pieces: You MUST roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto your starting square.

🔁 Bonus Roll: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent piece, or reaching center Home awards a bonus roll! (Max 3 consecutive sixes).

⚔️ Capturing: Land on an opponent's token to send it back to their yard, earning an immediate bonus roll!

⭐️ Safe Zones: Star squares (★) and starting squares are safe—tokens cannot be captured there.

🤖 Modes: Play AI vs Me with 1, 2, or 3 bots, or play offline Local Pass & Play with friends!
