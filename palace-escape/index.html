HOW TO PLAY

  1. Reach the glowing palace door to escape each level.
  2. Swipe on the maze to move (on a computer, use Arrow keys or WASD). Turn early — you'll take it at the next junction.
  3. Caught? No lives lost. Hit Restart Level and try again — as many times as you like.
  4. Grab power-ups from the first level. They activate automatically when touched.
  5. Every 10 levels earns an Aasthi Pathralu with more palace wealth for a faster run.
